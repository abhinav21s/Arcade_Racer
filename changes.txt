Neon Arcade Racer – Priority Improvements

HIGH PRIORITY (Game Feel)
- Tighter, more responsive steering + snappy acceleration
- Better drifting: keep speed, stronger trail, score bonus while drifting
- Camera: slight lag + zoom with speed
- Speed fantasy: motion blur / speed lines at high speed
- Crash: screen shake + particles, instant restart

MEDIUM PRIORITY (Missing Features)
- Power-ups: Nitro, Shield, Score Multiplier, Shockwave, Time Slow, Magnet, Overdrive
- Near-miss detection: big score + strong feedback on close passes
- Better traffic/obstacles that force decisions
- Combo/style multiplier for continuous drifts + near-misses
- Synthwave music + strong engine/drift/crash sounds
- Particles: neon drift trails, boost particles, crash bursts

VISUAL POLISH
- Stronger neon glow on car + road
- Better pseudo-3D road (curves/hills if possible)
- More roadside elements (buildings, lights, barriers)
- Screen shake on boost + crash
- Clear HUD: score, multiplier, speed, active power-up
- Better unlock feedback when new car unlocks

META / PROGRESSION
- Save high scores (localStorage)
- Clearer unlocked car display
- Simple car differences (trail color or handling)
- Optional later: daily/weekly modifiers

UX / MENU
- Selected car more obvious (pulse/glow)
- Instant “Play again” after crash

BUILD ORDER
1. Driving + drift feel
2. Visual juice (trails, shake, speed lines)
3. Near-miss + combo scoring
4. Power-ups
5. Sound
6. Unlocks + HUD + high scores
